NFC.

Payment infrastructure on a chip the size of a fingernail. ByteCoins was supposed to be a small project; I went down the rabbit hole on tokenization, secure-element architecture, and admin-only writing flows. Real money + plastic cards is a beautiful, terrifying problem.

Vibe coding.
